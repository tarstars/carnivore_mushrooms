Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: a finished SIX-PANEL wordless comic, portrait 2:3, exactly two columns by three rows with clear gutters.
Concept: "The dissertation that sprouted" — a dry scientific title escapes its catalogue and grows into a collective poem. Kind, intelligent absurd comedy, bright colours, exuberant storytelling. This is an invented archival fable; the protagonist is not a portrayal of a named real scientist.
Visual style: confident expressive pen line with luminous flat gouache and screenprint textures, contemporary literary comic, imaginative perspective, beautifully designed but easy to read. Palette warm sunflower yellow, coral, sky blue, jade green, paper white, touches of cobalt. Human warmth and humour, no ominous atmosphere.
Consistent protagonist in panels 1–4: a young fictional female archivist in a mustard cardigan with short dark hair and round glasses, expressive but not caricatured.
Panel 1 upper left: the archivist in a lively sunlit archive opens one enormous catalogue drawer and discovers a single unassuming cream research booklet.
Panel 2 upper right: as she opens it, a few large black Cyrillic-like letter SHAPES wriggle out as curling stems; the page corners become delicate fungal loops. Her delight is comic, not fear. No readable lettering.
Panel 3 middle left: she offers the growing page to three neighbours — a child, an old librarian and a bicycle mechanic — and each adds a small pen stroke. Their distinct coloured strokes connect and branch into one living garland of words.
Panel 4 middle right: the garland now links the open windows of a bright courtyard, people laughing and contributing ink strokes from different windows. The original archivist is visible, tiny, at one window. A community creating together, not an infestation.
Panel 5 lower left: jump into a playful microscopic view: a smooth tiny amber roundworm meets a three-lobed turquoise loop growing from a handwritten letter; fantastical, clearly drawn as a visual joke. The loop gently frames a tiny open book. No anatomy labels, no scientific measurements, no violence.
Panel 6 lower right: a large open book in the courtyard has become a lush garden of paper mushrooms and branching lines. People sit around its edges reading together. At the very foreground, a hand with a pen approaches one remaining blank page, inviting the viewer to join.
Constraints: exactly six coherent panels, readable sequence, maintain character identity and clothing, NO speech balloons, NO captions or readable text, NO panel numbers inside the art, NO author imitation, NO watermark. All text will be supplied outside the artwork. No monster faces, no attacks, no gloomy sepia or black wash. The comic's main protagonist is collective imagination itself.

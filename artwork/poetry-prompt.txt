Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a second, distinctly poetic fine-art poster for the project "Хищные грибы Узбекистана", portrait 3:4, richly textured print.
Primary request: portray the missing cultural layer: a real dry 1975 scientific title became an unruly collective internet poem. This is a literary fantasy, a visual chorus, not a scientific illustration. Central metaphor: language itself germinates, spreads through its readers, and catches them in rhyme.
Scene and composition: a small old typewritten scientific pamphlet lies open in a vast ink-dark space. Its loose ivory pages rise, curl, overlap and grow into a whole nocturnal CHORUS of strange paper mushrooms. Fungal caps are formed from folded, torn and rolled book pages, with organic black-ink gills underneath. Their thin paper stems branch into handwritten tendrils and mycelium, joining many different scraps into one living text. They should seem to listen and whisper together, no literal human faces. One larger curved paper mushroom in the middle anchors an ascending spiral of smaller ones. A very fine gold filament passes through a small three-part organic ring near the book, almost a punctuation mark. One tiny distant turquoise dome and ochre horizon suggest the Uzbek setting without dominating. The branching book-pages and fungal forest carry the eye upward like a song acquiring more voices. The image should feel learned, funny, quietly sinister and moving at the same time.
Medium and visual language: expressive hand-cut linocut, lithography and torn-paper collage, matte irregular ink, distressed library-paper fibres, a few fine letterpress impressions, sophisticated early twentieth-century book-art energy and contemporary literary poster typography. Bold silhouettes, deep blacks, restrained monumental rhythm. Not glossy 3-D, not a science infographic, not a decorative fantasy wallpaper.
Palette: ink black, old-paper ivory, cinnabar red, restrained oxidised turquoise and tiny touches of tarnished gold. Generous black negative space. Strong paper-light against darkness.
Typography: exact Cyrillic title at the top, in three large dynamic, beautifully spaced letterpress lines:
"ХИЩНЫЕ"
"ГРИБЫ"
"УЗБЕКИСТАНА"
In the lower quarter a clean, exceptionally legible three-line poem fragment, in a quiet warm-ivory serif, integrated with the image but with enough empty space:
"Рифма — тоже ловчее кольцо."
"Сначала ищешь слово."
"Потом оно не отпускает."
At the very bottom, small text:
"Поэтическая фантазия"
These exact words are original project text apart from the supplied concept title. Do not add further readable prose, fake author names, dates or invented quotations; page fragments may have abstract ink marks and broken unrecognisable glyphs.
Quality constraints: tactile, surprising, evocative, art-print quality. The motif must be many voices growing out of one title, not one angry mushroom character. No copied internet poem verses, no teeth, no gore, no flags, no mock ethnographic symbols, no photorealistic people, no watermark. Preserve the combination of absurdity, intellectual mischief, seduction and mild dread.

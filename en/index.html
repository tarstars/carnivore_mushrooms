One title.
Ten ways
to grow.

It was a scientific title. Then it became a shared refrain. Here it keeps changing shape: inviting you to tea, learning to dance, escaping a form, and asking you to become its next voice.

Ten experiments: look, read, listen, play, make. Four more studies and two films follow. Begin wherever your curiosity takes you. Original artwork accompanies the English reading and print editions.

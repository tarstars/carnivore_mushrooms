The worm follows dinner, a sweet little thread;
the table is laid in the soil ahead.
Who planted a letter before spring began?
Those hungry fungi of Uzbekistan.

The shelves are quiet; the lamps burn low,
a footnote is learning which way to grow.
Who kept the spare moon in a watering can?
The hungry fungi of Uzbekistan!

The manuscript settled. The ink settled too.
The letters, however, had work left to do.
Who stayed on the page when the daylight ran?
Those hungry fungi of Uzbekistan.

The worm makes a question. The fungus replies.
The answer is small, with a talent for surprise.
Who added a window to the afternoon plan?
Those hungry fungi of Uzbekistan.

When Tashkent settles into the blue,
a word leaves its page and comes looking for you.
Who poured one more cup than the teapot can?
Those hungry fungi of Uzbekistan.

The council assembled to classify tea;
the title escaped through a crack at half three.
Who folded a doorway into the plan?
Those hungry fungi of Uzbekistan.

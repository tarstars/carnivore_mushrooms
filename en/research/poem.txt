Rhyme, too, is a hunting ring

The catalogue was quiet.
Tashkent. Nineteen seventy-five.
Twenty-seven pages—
and a whole night between the lines.

Someone read the title out.
Another offered it a rhyme.
A third could not shut the window in time:
hyphae were already reaching out of letters.

Predatory fungi of Uzbekistan.

Each line found a voice.
Each voice found a neighbour.
We thought we were writing about a mycelium.
It had been writing through us for years.

Rhyme, too, is a hunting ring.
First you look for a word.
Then it
will not let go.

English rendering of the original project poem, 2026.
A new response to the collective-poem tradition, not a translation of the historical collective poem.
